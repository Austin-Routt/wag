**What a formula identifies.** An exception blocks what its terms name, and
no more finely (ChatGPT's review, its fixture 15). The engine knows a
statement by its formula and reads no case, time, place or occasion into a
term's words. rule_excepted(A,B) blocks every argument from A to B by that
rule. conflicting_or_overriding_rule(R,C) and rule_exception_or_excuse(R,C)
block every application of R that concludes C: two applications of one
rule with one conclusion, written alike for two cases, share one exception,
and an exception made out for one defeats both; through the bridge, a
waiver of R for the case X that takes effect defeats R's application to
every conclusion C about X. excuse_or_overriding_duty(A,X) is scoped to
the agent and the act, not to a rule or an occasion: an excuse of A for X
undercuts every regulative-rule argument that A must do X, whatever rule
or occasion it cites. Where two applications must be told apart, the
conclusion bears its case (C names the case, as example 6's does: "the
board must record a zero for Ana's missed paper", not "a zero must be
recorded"), R names the rule as it holds where and when it applies, or
the actor form is used, whose exceptions name the case, the rule, the
agent and the action. Where that is not enough, the distinction needs a
richer model of an application, which no scheme here gives.

### 1. A card payment and the plumber's records

**as an argument**, with `defeasible_modus_ponens`; our own text.

> Maria paid the plumber by card, and a card payment, as a rule, leaves a record with the one who is paid; so the plumber's records show her payment.

Bindings: A = “Maria paid the plumber by card”; B = “the plumber's records show Maria's payment”.

- If “Maria paid the plumber by card” is true then presumably “the plumber's records show Maria's payment” is also true.
- Maria paid the plumber by card.
- *Unless:* The defeasible rule from “Maria paid the plumber by card” to “the plumber's records show Maria's payment” has an exception in this case.
- **So:** The plumber's records show Maria's payment.

Pressed with its questions:

- **walton/rule_excepted**, at the exception “The defeasible rule from “Maria paid the plumber by card” to “the plumber's records show Maria's payment” has an exception in this case”: The plumber's card reader was offline that week and stored nothing: this payment falls outside the rule.
- **Challenge** (CQ:premise_acceptable), at the premise “If “Maria paid the plumber by card” is true then presumably “the plumber's records show Maria's payment” is also true”: A card payment leaves a record with the bank, not with every trader: many plumbers keep no books of their own.

*Note:* The exception names the rule's two ends, so the offline reader blocks this step and not another rule from the same fact (that the payment shows on Maria's bank statement, say). The challenge asks what backs the rule, the first of the two derived questions.
